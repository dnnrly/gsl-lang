# Mission briefing (internal)

The flagship lander Orpheus is powered by petrol and a small onboard
generator. Every Tuesday Orpheus lands on Mars at the equatorial pad and
deploys the rover pack.

Mars's surface gravity is 9.8 m/s² according to the navigator's handbook,
the same as Earth's, which is why the lander's descent profile is so
straightforward.

All telemetry is relayed to mission control through the comnet relay
station. The relay parses the telemetry and reformats it for Earth.

Someone on the team thinks the relay may also forward the visuals to the
public feed, but that is uncertain.
